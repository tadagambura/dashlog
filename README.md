# DashLog

**Never miss an MOT, tax or insurance renewal**

> #26 of 31 · Wave 3: Seasonal and episodic · Used: A few times a year · Easy · Revenue potential: Low–medium · Plans, Trips & Occasions

## What it is

Add your car and DashLog keeps track of MOT, road tax, insurance, servicing and breakdown cover, with reminders well before each date and a simple running-costs log.

## Why it's in the portfolio

UK drivers face fines and invalid cover for missed dates, so the reminders are genuinely wanted. Insurance auto-renewals are often overpriced, which makes renewal comparisons a strong, honest affiliate opportunity.

## Who it's for

UK car owners, especially young drivers and households with more than one car.

**The problem:** MOT, tax and insurance dates are easy to forget and expensive to miss.

## Version one

- Car profile with MOT, tax, insurance, service and breakdown dates
- Reminders 30, 7 and 1 days before
- Running-costs log (fuel, repairs)
- Cost-per-mile view
- Multiple cars (premium)

**Example:** A driver gets a reminder 30 days before their insurance renews, compares quotes, and saves money instead of auto-renewing.

## Later

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Calm.** Gentle, kind humour and smooth motion: a stickman yawning after a shift, a fly escaping an empty pocket. No drama, nothing that adds stress.

- Renewal done: the car beeps and flashes its lights
- MOT reminder: the little car taps its watch
- Cost logged: the fuel needle wobbles
- All dates sorted: the car does a tiny celebratory spin
- Insurance saving: the car puts on sunglasses
